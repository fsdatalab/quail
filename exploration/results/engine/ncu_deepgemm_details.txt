Speed-of-light of the real deep_gemm sm90_fp8_gemm_1d2d kernels at the
25,305-token step shapes, isolated, hardware counters (ncu, clock
control none). Averages over profiled launches per shape.

shape (N x K)        role   calls  Compute(SM)%  DRAM%   Duration us
  2560 x 4096          o     12         86.4   31.1        371.4
  2560 x 9728       down     11         90.9   31.6        875.8
  6144 x 2560        qkv      8         84.3   27.2        570.3

Caveats. The gate_up shape (19456 x 2560) was undersampled in the
render and its one parsed row paired with a down-shape metrics block
(its implied flop rate exceeds the hardware peak, which is
impossible); the kernel is structurally the down kernel at a larger
N and the well-sampled shapes bound it. Durations under ncu replay
run at boosted clocks and read faster than steady state: the
steady-state microbenchmark measured the gate_up call at 1,877 us
against these sub-900 us replay durations. The Compute (SM)
percentages are the robust column: the real kernels run at 84 to 91
percent of the math-unit ceiling in isolation, against about 70
percent effective in-pipeline by flop arithmetic - the gap is tail
waves and interleaving, not slow kernels.
