## Falsified today

- Expandable allocator segments was blamed for stock's wrong
  answers - wrongly. The re-runs WITHOUT the flag reproduce the
  same wrong counts (classifier 12,157 of 50,000; cliff 7,900,
  deterministic across repetitions). The numbers match the known
  4B fp8 kernel-numerics shift (~23.6 percent on this toolchain,
  notes/RESULTS.md). Something about these stock-only boots at
  0.88 selects the bad numeric path; open. Until resolved, the 4B
  stock classifier (164.6 s) and cliff stock (50.9 s) walls are
  not valid baselines - the cliff run even asks half the
  questions (14,459 against 28,468) because garbage answers fail
  the gate early.
- The reserve-plus-top-up admission design: a sibling delayed
  behind a top-up arrives after the document's first prompt
  finished, the document's KV is gone, and the sibling re-reads
  everything. Measured 3x worse (cap 16: 206.2 s, reads 2.347).
  Reverted to full charge at admission with release per finished
  prompt.
- "Stock boots die when sharing a container" - falsified; a
  stock-only fresh-container boot died the same way. It was the
  workspace OOM, and 0.88 fixes it.
